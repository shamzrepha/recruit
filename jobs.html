Photo hard to read? A Groq vision model can read it instead. That sends the image to our server, and only if you agree.

What happens next

  1. Your CV is read. On your device first. Nothing is sent to a recruiter yet.
  2. AI builds your profile: name, school, jobs, skills, laid out as a roadmap. You check it.
  3. You see roles ranked for you, how well your CV matches each, and where you would stand among applicants.
  4. You choose which roles to apply to.

Your phone and email are shared with a recruiter only if you tick the box.
