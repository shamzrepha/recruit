How a hire runs

  1. Brief the AI

    Chat about your startup and the role. You get requirements you can edit.

  2. Share the link

    Send candidates the job’s apply link. They upload a CV and confirm their details.

  3. Review the evidence

    Each requirement is Stated, Not stated or Contradicted, with the CV quote behind it.

  4. You decide

    Shortlist, ask first or pass. Then message the people you want to talk to.

Built to be fair and checkable

Consent first

Contact details are hidden unless the candidate agreed, and are left out of the CSV unless you switch them on.

No automatic rejections

The tool ranks and explains. A person makes every shortlist, hold and pass decision.

Evidence you can verify

Any quote the AI shows is checked against the CV text. If it is not there, the claim becomes “not stated”.

Built for a Talent Scout Intern application to Sourcing Sprints. Designed around the principles of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: consent, a clear purpose, minimum data and deletion on request. Candidate records expire after 30 days.
