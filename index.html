How a screen works

  1. Brief the AI

    Chat about your startup and the role. You get a requirements list you can edit.

  2. Add candidates

    Paste CVs or load people who applied through the candidate page.

  3. Review the evidence

    Each requirement is Stated, Not stated or Contradicted, with the CV quote behind it.

  4. You decide

    Shortlist, ask first or pass. Then message the people you want to talk to.

Built to be fair and checkable

Consent first

Candidate contact details are hidden unless the candidate agreed, and are left out of the CSV unless you switch them on.

No automatic rejections

The tool ranks and explains. A person makes every shortlist, hold and pass decision.

Evidence you can verify

Any quote the AI shows is checked against the CV text. If it is not there, the claim is downgraded to “not stated”.

Built for a Talent Scout Intern application to Sourcing Sprints. Designed around the principles of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: consent, a clear purpose, minimum data and deletion on request. Candidate records are set to expire after 30 days.
