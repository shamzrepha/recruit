Terms

Using Scout

Short and plain. By using Scout you agree to this.

What Scout is

Scout is a tool that helps candidates apply and helps employers read applications. It gives suggestions based on CV text. It is not a hiring agency and does not guarantee a job, an interview or a candidate.

Be honest

  • Only submit a CV that is yours, or that you have permission to submit.
  • Do not post fake jobs, ask candidates for money, or collect personal data you do not need.
  • Do not try to break, overload or scrape the site, or to read other people’s applications.

Employers

You are responsible for your hiring decisions. Treat the AI ranking as a starting point, check the evidence it shows, and do not use it as the only reason to reject someone. Follow the hiring and anti-discrimination laws where you operate.

Candidates

Match scores and “top %” figures are estimates based on the people who have applied so far. They can change and they are not a verdict on you.

No promises

Scout is provided as is. AI can be wrong. Data is deleted on the schedule in the privacy page, so keep your own copy of anything important. We may change or stop the service, and may remove jobs or accounts that break these rules.

Scout uses AI to suggest. A person decides.
