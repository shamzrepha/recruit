How it works

1

Upload a CV

PDF, photo or pasted text. Scout builds a clean profile: contact details, schools, jobs, skills and a career timeline.

2

See your fit

Every open role is scored against its requirements. You see what matches, what is missing, and roughly where you would rank.

3

Apply to the ones you want

Pick one role or five. Recruiters see ranked profiles, with the evidence from each CV quoted back.

Built so a person stays in charge

  • “Not on the CV” means verify, never reject.
  • Every score links to the line of the CV it came from.
  • Phone and email are hidden unless the candidate opts in.
  • Recruiters can ask, “show me the top 30”, and get a ranked list to open and review.
